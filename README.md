# Hazbin Hotel

**Hazbin Hotel** is an adult animated musical comedy-drama series created by **Vivienne Medrano** (*VivziePop*) and available on [Prime Video](https://primevideo.com).

---

## Premise

**Charlie Morningstar**, the Princess of Hell, opens a rehabilitation hotel called the **Hazbin Hotel** to help sinners redeem their souls and peacefully enter Heaven—offering an alternative to Heaven's annual *Extermination* to solve Hell's overpopulation.

> "Inside of every demon is a rainbow!" — *Charlie Morningstar*

---

## Main Characters

1. **Charlie Morningstar** – Optimistic Princess of Hell and founder of the Hazbin Hotel.
2. **Vaggie** – Charlie's fiercely loyal partner and the hotel's pragmatic manager.
3. **Angel Dust** – The hotel's very first guest, a famous adult film star with a guarded heart.
4. **Alastor (`The Radio Demon`)** – A powerful, mysterious Overlord who helps run the hotel for his own entertainment.
5. **Husk** – The grumpy, winged-cat bartender and former Overlord.
6. **Niffty** – The hyperactive, cleanliness-obsessed maid.

---

## Why I Love It

* **Broadway-Caliber Music:** Catchy original songs performed by a star-studded Broadway cast.
* **Expressive Animation:** Distinct, vibrant character designs and fluid musical sequences.
* **Redemption & Found Family:** Balances dark humor with genuine emotional growth among the residents.
